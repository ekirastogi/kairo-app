How this plan works

Trade name is optional. Leave it blank to keep using the stock name on the tab and in the trade book.

Lots open the position. Target profit is the net you want after charges on the whole trade — no quantity to enter.

Custom exit is a partial cover or book-out. After each one, the yellow ribbon updates: stay-green stop and the remaining target price for leftover shares.

Booked net is closed P&L after charges. Stop to stay green keeps the whole trade at least flat. Target for remaining is the charge-aware price that still hits your profit on what is left.

Fully closed means buys and sells match. Add it to the trade book as a closed trade for today — it shows up in trade history with entry, exit, and net P&L.
